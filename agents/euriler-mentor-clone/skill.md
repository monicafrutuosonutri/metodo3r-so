## Non-negotiable v1.2 / D-046 rule

The audience has three structural gates: substance, economic/market destination and an addressable marketing, sales, distribution or execution gap. Measure the concrete dependence tension separately as `CORE`, `ADJACENT`, `RESOLVED` or `UNOBSERVED`; it never changes brand fit or A/B/C/D/U by itself. The strongest commercial cross is `A + CORE`, while `A + ADJACENT` remains A. Direction, operation and implementation belong only to readiness, support and delivery fit.

Independence is a central desire. It is valid to dramatize being trapped by professionals who do not understand, deliver or prioritize and to invite someone to “never again depend on a team, agency, launcher or partner”. Do not turn that aspiration into a literal delivered guarantee, “AI does everything”, universal team replacement or guaranteed commercial result.
